STYLiSH — E-commerce Platform

Building a responsive e-commerce experience from product discovery to checkout.

Role
Front-End Developer & UX Designer
Scope
React, REST APIs, Responsive Design, E-commerce UX

01

Background

STYLiSH is a responsive e-commerce platform designed to support the complete shopping journey — from browsing and product discovery to account management and checkout. The project challenged me to think beyond individual screens and build a scalable front-end experience that connects user interaction, application state, backend data, and responsive behavior.

02

The challenge

E-commerce interfaces look simple until all of their states are considered. A single product experience needs to account for:

Product data→Variants→Inventory→Cart state→Authentication→Checkout

At the same time, the experience had to remain intuitive across desktop and mobile. My challenge was translating these interconnected requirements into a usable, maintainable front-end architecture.

03

Development

I built the product using JavaScript and React, integrating REST APIs to dynamically retrieve and render product information:

Product discovery

Responsive product browsing and category navigation.

Product details

Dynamic product information and purchasing options.

Shopping & membership

Account functionality supporting history, discounts, and user-specific experiences.

Responsive UX

Layouts optimized across desktop and mobile environments.

04

Extended commerce experiences

I also explored how commerce could move beyond a traditional product catalog: a real-time customer chat channel between shoppers and merchants, a live-commerce concept for livestream shopping with simultaneous seller communication, and real-time product ranking built around dynamically changing sales information. These helped me think about e-commerce not only as a transaction funnel, but as a combination of content, community, discovery, and conversion.
